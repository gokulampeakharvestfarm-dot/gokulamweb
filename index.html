Our Mission

A safe home built on compassion.

Gokulam Goshala is dedicated to providing cows with protection, nourishment, shelter and loving care.

Our goal is to create a peaceful environment where every cow can live with dignity while encouraging the community to participate in meaningful seva.

Learn about our seva →

What We Do

Care that begins with love.

Every part of our work is focused on the wellbeing, safety and dignity of the cows.

🌿

Healthy Nourishment

Providing nutritious food, fresh water and a clean environment for healthy living.

♡

Daily Care

Regular attention, shelter and compassionate care throughout every stage of life.

✚

Medical Support

Veterinary support and timely treatment whenever a cow needs special attention.

♧

Seva & Awareness

Bringing people together through volunteering, education and community participation.
