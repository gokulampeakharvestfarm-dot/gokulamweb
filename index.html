About Gokulam

A shelter where every cow matters.

Gokulam Goshala is dedicated to providing a safe, clean and caring environment for cows. Our work is rooted in compassion, responsible care and service.

From daily feeding and shelter maintenance to health care and community involvement, every small act of seva helps create a better life for the cows entrusted to us.

How we serve

Care that begins with compassion

Our daily work focuses on the essentials that keep the Goshala healthy and sustainable.

🌾

Food & Fodder

Safe Shelter

Clean, comfortable spaces designed for rest, movement and protection.

Health Care
